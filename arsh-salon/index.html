Our Story

Welcome to Arsh's

Located in the heart of Undri, Pune, Arsh's Unisex Salon is a premium, inclusive space dedicated to elevating your personal style. Whether you are looking for a classic gentleman's fade, a transformative hair color, or a relaxing bespoke grooming ritual, our highly skilled stylists are here to deliver excellence.

We believe that true luxury lies in the details. From our curated selection of top-notch professional products to our warm, welcoming atmosphere, every aspect of your visit is designed to leave you feeling confident, refreshed, and empowered.
